- Billing info: processed by payment processors; we do not store full credit card numbers.
- Public content such as comments.
- Survey and feedback responses.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically
- Usage and learning progress data (lessons started/completed, quiz results).
- Product interaction data, such as screens visited, features used, taps, and session activity.
- Device, app, diagnostic, performance, and limited network telemetry data.
- Cookie, mobile SDK, and similar identifier data.
- Session replay data that helps us understand how the Service is used and diagnose problems. We apply privacy controls designed to avoid capturing sensitive inputs. In our mobile app, text inputs and images are masked, and application logs and network request and response bodies are not collected.

2.3 Information from Third Parties

5. Analytics, Session Replay & Similar Technologies
------------------------------------------------------------

We use Cookies, mobile software development kits (SDKs), and similar technologies to provide functionality, track learning progress, support analytics, and diagnose technical problems.

We use PostHog as an analytics service provider. PostHog may process account identifiers, including user ID, email address, and username; product interactions and session activity; device, app, diagnostic, and performance information; membership status; and session replay data. This information may be associated with your account so that we can understand how people use the Service and improve it.

Session replay is designed to show navigation and interactions within the Service. We apply privacy controls designed to avoid capturing sensitive inputs. In our mobile app, text inputs and images are masked, and application logs and network request and response bodies are not collected. We do not use PostHog data for cross-app advertising and do not sell it to data brokers.
